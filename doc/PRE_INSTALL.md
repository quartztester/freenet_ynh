**Before installing, two things to know:**

- This app **volunteers your server's bandwidth and disk** as a peer of the Freenet decentralized network: it stores and forwards encrypted content for the network. Traffic is end-to-end encrypted at rest and in transit; still, check that your ISP's terms of service tolerate P2P-style relay traffic before proceeding.
- The node reaches peers over UDP port **31337 (default)**. Forward it on your router to this server, or the node works but with degraded connectivity.
